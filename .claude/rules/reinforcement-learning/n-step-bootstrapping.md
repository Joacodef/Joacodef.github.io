# The n-step bootstrapping note: its run and numbers

Its text's example, the hallway, is in `hallway.md`.

- **Section 3: the 4-step Sarsa figure.** n = 4, α = 0.5, γ = 1, ε = 0.1, seed 2606746, 30 episodes, 913 moves. Episode 1, played move by move: 18 moves, A1 left twice and down (random) into the wall, up to A2, right to B2 (moves 1 to 3 wait for rewards, move 4 makes the first update, Q(A1, left) 0 → −2), then the first fall at move 6, down from B2, which enters the target of A1 down from τ = 2 (0 → −51.5); over the next three moves the fall reaches back into A1 up, A2 right and B2 down, each to −51.5, and the agent goes on through row 3 to the goal. With a short first episode, a fall that lands in a path move's target after the path settles is very rare (1 seed in 300,000), so the figure's "A good move pays for a fall" may come before the settling: here in episode 28 (A1 up, A2 down at random, A1 right at random, and a fall), which drops A1 up from −12.16 to −63.5 while the best moves follow the top row, and leaves them without a route for a while. The best moves settle on the top row (11 moves) in episode 29; 13 falls, none after; the final values along it are −18.08, −12.04, −10, −8.86, −10.27, −7.01, −5.5, −4, −3, −2, −1, all at or below minus the moves left.
- **Pseudocode beside the 4-step Sarsa figure** ("n-step Sarsa, in each episode"; lines numbered 1 to 6, two loop headers kept within two levels of indent, the deepest `notes.css` draws): 1 S₀ ← the start, A₀ ← ε-greedy from Q, T ← ∞ (pick); *For t = 0, 1, 2, …, until τ = T − 1:* 2 If t < T: take A_t; observe R_t+1, S_t+1 (take); 3 If S_t+1 is terminal: T ← t + 1 (plain); 4 Else: A_t+1 ← ε-greedy from Q (pick); *τ ← t − n + 1; if τ ≥ 0:* 5 G ← the sum of discounted rewards, plus γⁿQ(S_τ+n, A_τ+n) if τ + n < T (target); 6 the update of Q(S_τ, A_τ) (update). Presses: the first choice lights 1; each take 2 (the last move of episode 1 plays 2 then 3); each next choice 4; a target 5; an update 6. The update of move 5 and the two later presses of "Episode 1 ends" give their target in the readout, so they play 5 then 6 in phases (the window, then the pair in green), three times over for the last three moves updated after the goal. A whole move (from "The fall reaches back" to move 17) plays 2, 4, 5, 6 in phases drawn from the take, next-choice, target and update builds, the next move staying yellow through 5 and 6. In "A good move pays for a fall", the press after the fall plays 4 then 2 (the next move chosen in yellow, then taken). The two summary slides light nothing; a skip runs each move's own pass: 1 at an episode's first move, 2, then 3 at its last move or 4 otherwise, and 5, 6 for each update the move makes (none in the first three moves, four at the last). Short texts: the first choice and the next choices of moves 1 to 3 give only the reason (and that no update comes yet); the updates of move 4, the first fall and "A good move pays for a fall" stand alone ("toward its target", without "Still" or "So"), and that slide's target names the fall's move ("The fall at move 3"). The Spanish panel keeps "n-step Sarsa" and calls A1 "el inicio", as the grid's label does.
