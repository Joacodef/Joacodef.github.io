# The control with approximation note: its figures and numbers

- The book's chapter 10 has two inconsistencies, pointed out to Joaquín: (10.14) writes R̄ with a time index, R̄_{t+n−1}, while the box uses one current R̄ for all n rewards, and it keeps the episodic "t + n < T, otherwise G_t" in a setting with no T. Mountain Car, the book's example, is not used: the note stays on the class's cart-pole.
- The agent sees twelve situations: the six ranges of the angle and which way the pole swings (θ̇ ≤ 0 left, > 0 right), with a weight for each situation and push, 24 in all. The angle alone does not do: in `stepCP`'s Euler step the next angle is θ + τθ̇ with the old θ̇, so the next state's range does not depend on the push, one-step Sarsa's target has the same expectation for both pushes, and its fixed point gives them equal weights (an angle-only run that seemed to learn did so only through lag). Its drawings, `pushChart` and `createPushView`, are described in `figure-modules.md`; in figure readouts the weights are named w1 to w24, panel by panel, swinging left first (`featNo`).
- Section 2's slideshow: the first push in six presses (start, choose, take, choose A′, the TD error, the update), steps 2 to 9 one press each. A skip plays the episode it lands on, step by step, not the last states of the episodes it passes, which once the pole stays up all look alike (in the mockup the cart sat at the right end, which looked unnatural to Joaquín); the runs are computed twice, first to find the episodes the slides stop at, then to keep their steps, and the skip's note says the drawing shows the last episode. Section 5's slideshow plays the last 200 steps before each stop the same way. The slide where the pole stops falling is titled "The pole stays up, but not the cart" (under "The pole stays up" the cart moved right). Figure 1 and Figure 4 are drags: the pole's tip (its angle in steps of 1° between ±0.5° and ±11.5°, so never on a range's edge, the swing set by the direction it last moved) and the end of the running sum of R − R̄ (R̄ in steps of 0.0005).
- **The numbers (text and figures),** computed by `rl/ctl-*.mjs` in the c9da7854 scratchpad. Twelve situations, 24 weights. The best policy over the angle alone, push toward the lean, lasts 42 steps; over the twelve situations the best found lasts about 202, and every such policy loses the cart, around 180 to 200 steps. Figure 1: the random policy's average return after each push, 400,000 episodes (seed 424242), left and right made symmetric: falling right 4° to 8°, right 12.66 and left 7.49 (w22, w21); every falling situation prefers the push the way the pole falls. Figure 2: one-step semi-gradient Sarsa, ε = 0, every weight 300, α = 0.1 fixed, seed 4416, 500 episodes. Episode 1 takes 9 steps: a tie broken left at 0.24° (swinging left), left again and again (ties at A1, A2, A6, A7, greedy at A3 to A5) while the pole swings right, a tie broken right past 8°, and the fall drops w24 from 300 to 270.1. Episode 2 falls the same way and tries left past 8°: 270.19. Episode 8 is the first long one, 173 steps, the cart off the left end. Weights first creep above 300 (up to 308.8, a push kept in one situation has TD error 1); the last falls below 300 after episode 81, and after 100 none is above 279; episode 100 is the run's longest, 286 steps (cart), while 47 of episodes 51 to 100 end with the pole falling. From episode 207 the pole never falls: 294 episodes, all ending with the cart off the right end after 111 to 271 steps (mean 184), the pole leaning right over each second half (median 3.3°), the cart at 1.1 to 2.4 m/s; in the final weights the two mirror situations near upright, 0° to 4° swinging left and −4° to 0° swinging right, both push left. At the end, each falling situation prefers the push the way it falls by 37 to 119 steps. With the prediction note's shrinking α, 100 runs average 68 steps over their last 100 of 500 episodes, against 182 with α fixed (none settles, against 98). Figure 3: n-step semi-gradient Sarsa with Figure 2's settings, 200 agents, 300 episodes, n = 1, 4, 16, 64: the first 50 episodes average 38.7, 90.1, 137.0 and 105.8 steps (n = 2: 60.4, 8: 123.5, 32: 125.4, 128: 93.5); curves drawn as 10-episode running means. Figure 5: differential semi-gradient 4-step Sarsa on the continuing cart-pole, α = 0.1, β = 0.0001 (with 0.001, R̄ swung between −0.0087 and −0.0034), ε = 0, w and R̄ from 0, seed 904, 100,000 steps: the first failure at step 10 (the pole, left), its −1 in the windows of steps 7 to 10, R̄ 0 → −0.0004; 18 failures by step 1,000 (15 the pole's), R̄ −0.0059; the pole's last fall at step 1,509, the 27th failure and 22nd fall; 74 failures by 10,000 (R̄ −0.0061), 568 by 100,000 (R̄ −0.0055), 109 in the last 20,000 steps, one every 183, all 541 after step 1,509 the cart off the right end; the falling situations prefer the push the way it falls by 0.1 to 0.8. Figure 4: 1,100 steps of Figure 5's final policy from a fresh start (seed 27), the cart leaving at steps 173, 335, 509, 671, 882 and 1050, so the sum stays level at R̄ = −0.0055.
- **Figure 2's pseudocode** ("Episodic semi-gradient Sarsa", "Semi-gradient Sarsa para tareas episódicas"), the steps of section 2 with the update split into the TD error and the step, as the readouts write it: under "Repeat, for each episode:", 1 S ← the first state, 2 A ← ε-greedy from q̂(S, ·, w); under "Repeat, for each step:", 3 take A, observe R, S′, 4 if S′ is terminal, w ← w + α[R − q̂(S, A, w)]∇q̂(S, A, w), next episode, 5 A′ ← ε-greedy from q̂(S′, ·, w), 6 δ ← R + γq̂(S′, A′, w) − q̂(S, A, w), 7 w ← w + αδ∇q̂(S, A, w), 8 S ← S′, A ← A′ (the panel's numbers; in `code` the indices count the two heads). The first slide's six presses light 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7; each of steps 2 to 8 plays 8, 3, 5, 6, 7 and the fall 8, 3, 4, in phases (the push about to be taken in yellow, the cart moved, A′ in yellow, the target's push in red, the update); the slides after a skip light 4, the update that ends an episode. A skip's pass is 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, or 3, 4, 4, 4, 1 at an episode's end (five slots each, since the skip picks a line by its place in the pass and can switch passes partway; the ending pass leaves out line 2 so that a new episode's line never lights in the middle of a step). Short texts: A′'s and the target's presses of the first slide, the plain greedy steps (the weight said in full), the fall, and the last slide's second press.
- **Figure 5's pseudocode** ("Differential semi-gradient <i>n</i>-step Sarsa", the name kept in Spanish too, with the note's italic n), the steps of section 5: 1 S₀ ← the first state, 2 A₀ ← ε-greedy from q̂(S₀, ·, w); under "For t = 0, 1, 2, …:", 3 take A_t, observe R_t+1, S_t+1, 4 A_t+1 ← ε-greedy from q̂(S_t+1, ·, w); under "If τ = t − n + 1 ≥ 0:", 5 δ ← Σ(R_i − R̄) + q̂(S_τ+n, A_τ+n, w) − q̂(S_τ, A_τ, w), 6 R̄ ← R̄ + βδ, w ← w + αδ∇q̂(S_τ, A_τ, w). The start lights 1, the first push 2; steps 1 to 3 play 3, 4, steps 4 to 9 and 11 to 13 play 3, 4, 5, 6, and the failure 3, 4, 5, 6 (A_t+1 chosen from the fresh start, its bar yellow on the fallen cart), in phases (the cart moved, the next push in yellow, the window dashed blue with the target's push in red, the update); the slides after a skip light 6, and a skip's pass is 3, 4, 5, 6. Short texts: the three presses of "A failure" after the failure itself, which leaned on it ("still in the window").
