# The DDPG note (Paper 2)

Lillicrap et al., "Continuous control with deep reinforcement learning" (ICLR 2016). Sources: the paper's checked companion `course-materials/reinforcement-learning/papers/DDPG.md` (formulas, Algorithm 1, settings, numbered slips), the plan `reference/ddpg-plan.md`, the specs `scripts/ddpg/note/spec-part1.md` and `spec-full.md`, and the decisions log `reference/paper2-progress.md`.

## Joaquín's decisions (Oct 2026)

- Fun: no long paragraphs; one figure kind in every section, the rally player (the game plays, Play/Pause, automatic pauses with a short card and Continue); per section only the reel and the pauses change; mode buttons where a section compares runs; the one other interaction is tapping the critic's curve in section 1, which plays the tapped speed for one frame and then the actor.
- The page opens with a short introduction (no formula, about 120 words) before the first player, and every section opens with a sentence or two before its player ("I don't like that it immediately starts with the animation", 21 Oct 2026).
- No reference to DQN, Paper 1 or the DQN note anywhere a reader sees (paper notes stand alone); links only to the chapter notes. Theory and algorithm, not the paper's results.
- "frame", never "screen" (Spanish "cuadro").
- The game is drawn continuous, not as a pixel grid (Joaquín's decision of 21 Oct 2026, replacing whole lit cells): the agent reads five numbers, so the field is a plain area in `--img-lo`, the ball a round dot and the paddle a bar in `--img-hi` at its real top, both gliding between recorded frames. No grid lines or cells.
- More information, always alive (same decision): a yellow speed arrow on the paddle at every frame, and, at the top of every player's right column, a speed gauge and the five numbers the agent reads, updated every frame. Section 2 plays a long stretch of training in fast-forward while the critic's curve for one remembered frame is redrawn from the recorded critic.
- Motion: a player autoplays when at least half of it is in view (Joaquín's exception), except with prefers-reduced-motion, where nothing starts by itself and every player shows Play. There is no switch in the URL.

## The game and the runs

- The rally with any speed (`rally.js`): 16 × 12 pixels, the ball one pixel moving one cell diagonally per frame, a paddle of 3 pixels in column 15 whose top py is a real number in [0, 9], moved each frame by the speed a ∈ [−1, 1] (down positive); +1 for a hit, −1 and the end of the episode for a miss, minus a cost of moving 0.05 a² per frame; γ = 0.95; 24 serves; a check every 1,000 steps plays the 24 serves greedily, and "perfect" means each serve played to the tenth hit (the cut).
- The note's run: seed 3, networks of 32 and 32 units, critic rate 3e-4, actor rate 1e-4, τ = 0.005, minibatch 64, memory 100,000, updates from step 64, Ornstein–Uhlenbeck noise θ = 0.15, σ = 0.2 (𝒩_t = 0.85 𝒩_{t−1} + 0.2 n_t, spread 0.38), batch normalization on, 20,000 steps (`../rally/lib/`, never edited).
- Scripts (outside the repository, in `course-materials/reinforcement-learning/scripts/ddpg/note/`): `gen-data.mjs` (opening and section 1, checked by `check-data.mjs`), `gen-train.mjs` with `train-lib.mjs` (sections 2–6, checked by `check-train.mjs`, which retrains the traced runs bit for bit against `../rally/reruns/runs/*.json`, replays every reel through the game's rules and recounts every ten-seed figure), `c0-run.mjs` (no cost of moving), and `write-site-data.mjs`, which writes `deep-deterministic-policy-gradient-data.js` and rewrites the `?v=` of its import. Ten-seed counts come only from `../rally/reruns/` (seeds 1–10, 20,000 steps, `results.md`).

## The paper's numbers quoted (from `DDPG.md`)

More than 20 simulated physics tasks; Lillicrap and seven colleagues at DeepMind; τ = 0.001; minibatch 64 for low-dimensional inputs; memory 10⁶; layers of 400 and 300 units; learning rates 10⁻⁴ (actor) and 10⁻³ (critic); γ = 0.99; OU noise θ = 0.15, σ = 0.2. Slips used: 9 (no terminal case in y_i), 19 and 41 (μ′ and θ each mean two things), 42 (no step for the OU process), 24 (s′_i for s_{i+1}).
