# The eligibility traces note: its examples, run and numbers

Its first example, the hallway, is in `hallway.md`.

- **The room.** A 5 × 4 room, a first episode of nine moves (cells (0,3), (1,3), (1,2), (0,2), (0,1), (1,1), (2,1), (2,0), (3,0), (4,0), row 0 at the top), reward +1 only on reaching the charger, all values 0, γ = 1, α = 0.5. One-step Sarsa raises the last move to 0.5, 4-step Sarsa the last four, and Sarsa(λ) with λ = 0.9 every move, to 0.5 · 0.9^k for the move k steps before the end (0.5 down to 0.22).
- **Section 5: the Sarsa(λ) figure.** Accumulating traces, α = 0.1, λ = 0.9, γ = 1, ε = 0.1, seed 757123, 30 episodes, 643 moves. Episode 1, played move by move: 19 moves, A1 down twice into the wall (move 2 the second visit, z = 1.9), A1 up, A2 left into the wall, A2 down, A1 left twice into the wall, the first fall at move 8 (right from A1: all 6 traced moves drop at once, each by its trace, A1 left most, −17.1, then A1 down, −10.1), the same fall at move 9 (right chosen before the update counted the first one, δ = −101.06), A1 down at random, A1 up, and along row 2 to the goal. The best moves settle one row up (9 moves, the Sarsa figure's route) in episode 14; final values −11.45, −10.33, −8.9, −7.83, −6.06, −4.98, −3.62, −2.27, −0.96; 6 falls, none after settling.
- **The Sarsa(λ) figure's pseudocode** (titled "Sarsa(λ)", as the note names the method): under "Repeat, for each episode:", 1 z(s, a) ← 0 for every pair; S ← the first state, 2 A ← ε-greedy from Q in S; under "Repeat, for each move, until S is terminal:", 3 take A; observe R, S′, 4 A′ ← ε-greedy from Q in S′, 5 δ ← R + γQ(S′, A′) − Q(S, A) (red), 6 z(S, A) ← z(S, A) + 1, 7 Q(s, a) ← Q(s, a) + αδz(s, a), for every pair, 8 z(s, a) ← γλz(s, a), for every pair, 9 S ← S′; A ← A′ (the per-pair lines say "for every pair" rather than sitting under a third loop head, which the panel's styles do not indent). Presses: the opening lights 1; move 1's choice 2; each take 3, after 9 when the press before did not play it (moves 2 to 6: the grid shows the move chosen in S, then the move); the choice of A′ 4; the TD error 5 then 6 (the red mark first, then the trace of the move just made growing); the update 7 then 8 (the values change, then the green traces fade by 0.9, which is how the press ends); the first fall's recalled choice 9, its grid the table after the step before's update (the reason still read from the table the choice was made from); each move in one press (move 7, and from the second fall to the end of episode 1) plays 9, 3, 4 (unless it ends the episode), 5, 6, 7, 8 as phases from the one-line builds, about 1.2 s, A′ in yellow and any fall's ring kept from line 4 to the end so that the last phase equals the build (the wall mark of a bump shows only during the take), the take held for two line-times so that the glide or the fall plays out; "A path one row up" lights 7 (its skip ends on the update that settles the route), "After 30 episodes" lights nothing; a skip plays one pass per move: 1 and 2 on an episode's first move, then 3 to 9, without 4 and 9 on the move that ends an episode. With the panel, move 1's choice, TD error and update and every one-press move have short texts (the one-press moves give the TD error's value and leave what it does to line 7).
