# Note 7, subwords and fastText: its figures and numbers

Planned on 10 Oct 2026 from class 3, slides 9 to 15, and class 4, slide 2 (three drafted plans merged by a judge, in `course-materials/natural-language-processing/note7/judge.md`, with 16 decisions), and built without a mockup round: Joaquín asked for the notes to go ahead without his feedback. Its decisions follow the judge's recommendations, except where noted below. The scripts are in `note7/w7/`; `facts.txt` there holds every number below, written by `data7.mjs --facts`. A review by workflow (five angles: facts, theory and notation, the Spanish page, figures and code, the reader's path; each angle's findings checked by a verifier that tried to refute them) found 66 problems that held, many found from two or three angles, all fixed before publishing; its results are in `note7/w7/review7-raw.json`.

## Notation

- The slides' symbols: 𝒢_o (the runs of 3 to 6 characters of <o>, with o itself added when it has a row of its own), g, v_g, G = V + 2,000,000, e_g, x_o (multi-hot), W_in ∈ ℝ^{G×d}, W_out ∈ ℝ^{d×V}, u_c, s(o, c) = Σ_g u_cᵀv_g, σ, y, |Σ|, cos(v_o, v_{o′}). The page writes the slides' sum and says the program averages (it does in training too, adding the whole step to each row).
- h stays σ(s(o, c)), as the notes settled; the hash gets no letter (the summary's variable is `fnv`); K is not used. |o| is a word's length in characters, |Σ| the number of symbols, defined in words.
- Terms: subword (masculine in Spanish, «un subword»), tied once in section 2 to note 6's "pieces of words" («pedazos de palabras»); «piezas» is kept for BPE (note 10). Character n-gram / n-grama de caracteres (the slides' char n-grama, glossed once); run / tramo de n caracteres seguidos; boundary marks / marcas de borde; out of vocabulary (OOV) / fuera del vocabulario; compositionality / composicionalidad; multi-hot in English; a row of its own / fila propia; hash function / función de hash; shared start / inicio compartido, shared end / final compartido.
- The English text says "training runs" for the five trainings, since a "run" of characters is a subword on this page (the Spanish says «entrenamientos»); section 5 says the figures and the numbers quoted show the first training run and that the text claims only what held in at least four of the five.
- x_o ∈ {0, 1, 2, …}^G, not the slides' {0, 1}^G: a subword repeated in a word, or two subwords in one row, gives a 2 (slips 24). V is defined in section 4 as the number of words with a row of their own (4,993; 5259). "word2vec's original program" kept only W_in; fastText's .bin keeps W_out too, so the page says W_out plays no part in the word vectors. "Readings" names the passes over the text, as note 6 does, for the book's model and the paper's alike.

CBOW: each word around goes in by its subwords of 5 and its own row (are, not, but, windmills: 2 + 2 + 2 + 8 = 14; no, son, sino, molinos: 1 + 2 + 3 + 6 = 12), each word's rows averaged and weighted by its place (the published tables are CBOW with position weights), the target comes out whole; a word with no subword of 5 (we, no) gets the sentence about the zero vector. The counts are whole numbers (`localNum` passed to `map` once took the index for its decimals: "2 + 2.0 + 2.00 + 8.000"; `sweep7.mjs` now checks that figures 2 and 6's sums add up in whole numbers).

## Numbers

- The model, `sgns7.mjs`: note 6's `sgns.mjs` (d = 100, radius drawn from 1 to m = 5, k = 5 with β = ¾, 20 readings, t = 10⁻⁴, minimum count 5, rate 0.025) with only the input side split: each target goes in by its own row and its subwords of 3 to 6 characters of <word>, hashed by FNV-1a over UTF-8 bytes, each sign-extended, mod 2,000,000; the hidden vector is the mean of the rows, and the whole step is added to each row; rows no word with a vector uses keep a start uniform in ±1/d from a generator seeded by the bucket and the run. With subwords off it reproduces note 6's vectors byte for byte. Five runs, seeds 1 to 5; 101.2 s against 40.9 s without subwords (English), 97.7 against 38.0 (Spanish).
